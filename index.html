THE ROUTE TO THURSDAY

Return, then refine.

  1. Today · learn the shapes

    Try three 12-minute sessions, with breaks. Rotate left and right hands across different sections. Finish with one slow both-hands attempt.

  2. Wednesday · join the hands

    Play yesterday’s sections from the score before listening. Use Mix & combine; revisit the two weakest transitions. Raise tempo by 5 only after two clean returns.

  3. Thursday · play, don’t cram

    Warm up for 10 minutes. Test starts at measures 5 and 9, then one full run at a reliable tempo. Keep going after a small slip.

The MP3 recordings in this folder are copied without editing from the Tonejs/audio Salamander collection. This site selects a nearby sample, shifts its playback rate to the desired pitch, and applies volume, panning, and note-release envelopes at playback. Rendered WAV exports use these recordings under the same attribution requirement. See source-readme.txt and manifest.json for source information and file hashes.
